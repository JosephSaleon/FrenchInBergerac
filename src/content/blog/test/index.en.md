**At 8.30 am**, we have breakfast together. You tell me what you discovered the day before, what surprised or puzzled you, and what you’d like to see in the afternoon.

At 9 am, we start with a few gentle yoga stretches. It’s a chance to wake up your body and ease into the day before we begin your one-to-one French lesson, which runs until midday.

Before you arrive, we plan your lessons together around your level, goals and interests. A newspaper article might give us something to debate. A film clip might help you tune your ear to spoken French and pick up expressions you’ll hear in everyday life. Either way, I give you time to put your thoughts into words, then we go over any words or phrases you were looking for.

## From midday until 5 or 6 pm — Bergerac at your own pace

At midday, you can head upstairs to the flat to make yourself a bite to eat, or have lunch outside at a café or restaurant in town. Then the afternoon is yours. You might stop for a coffee, wander through the streets of old Bergerac, pop into a museum or a shop, or take a walk along the Dordogne. At the bakery, in the shops, at a restaurant or at the newsagent’s, you’ll find plenty of chances to try out the expressions we worked on that morning.

We can also adapt the timetable to the weather. If it’s very hot, we can switch things around so you can go for a walk or explore the town in the cool of the morning. I’ll meet you in the afternoon for your French lesson.

On days when you have the evening to yourself, you could take a trip by train to Bordeaux, Saint-Émilion or Sarlat, depending on the timetable.

## In the evening — An early dinner, then a trip to the cinema

When we’re going to the cinema, we have dinner together early enough to get there in time. I cook simple, homely meals using local produce and organic ingredients whenever possible. If you feel like it, we can cook dinner together. I’ll share a few of my favourite ways to bring out the flavour of a dish with fresh herbs from my garden. We’ll choose a film together, depending on what’s showing and what we’re in the mood for.

Fancy spending a few days in Bergerac practising your French? Write to me and tell me when you’d like to come, what you’d like to work on and what you’re interested in. **Together, we’ll plan a stay that suits you.**
